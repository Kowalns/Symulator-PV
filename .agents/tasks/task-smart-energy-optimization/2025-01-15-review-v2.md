# Smart energy optimization v2: fixing peak-discharge, pass-1 fidelity, and double-charge collision

The fix commit addresses the three blocking issues from v1: the autokonsumpcja peak-discharge no-op is replaced with active force-discharge logic that displaces PV for sale at peak prices; pass-1 now simulates peak-hour discharge for both modes, giving the grid-charge planner accurate remaining-capacity estimates; and hours with PV surplus are excluded from grid-charge planning via a `godziny_nadwyzki_pv` set. All 296 tests pass.

Watch for: the autokonsumpcja peak-discharge "freed PV" path double-counts energy — it sells production that was already accounted as autokonsumpcja in the same hour, inflating both `sprzedaz_kwh` and revenue (confirmed); in summer peak hours where PV is still producing, the battery charges from PV surplus then immediately discharges the same hour, losing roundtrip efficiency with no economic benefit (confirmed, low severity).

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The three v1 blocking issues are resolved. Pass 1 now mirrors pass 2's peak-hour discharge logic for both modes, so `brakujaca_energia` reflects realistic end-of-day battery state. The `godziny_nadwyzki_pv` set prevents grid-charge planning from picking hours where PV surplus would collide.

The autokonsumpcja force-discharge introduces a new accounting problem: when `bilans >= 0` at the peak hour, the code first counts `zuzycie` as PV autokonsumpcja and sells the surplus, then the peak-discharge block discharges battery to "cover" the same zuzycie and sells `nadwyzka_uwolniona = min(energia_dostarczona, produkcja)` as freed PV. The total energy attributed to sale + autokonsumpcja exceeds actual production for that hour, overstating economic savings.

When the peak hour falls within PV production hours (summer 16-18h), PV surplus charges the battery and peak discharge immediately empties it — the battery acts as a lossy pass-through (~5% roundtrip loss) with no benefit. Skipping charge when `g == godzina_rozladowania` would eliminate ~50-100 kWh/year of unnecessary loss.

## Double-counted energy at peak hour (autokonsumpcja mode)

The execution sequence in pass 2 for an hour where `bilans >= 0` AND `g == godzina_rozladowania`:

```
1. autokonsumpcja = zuzycie → counted in autokonsumpcja_kwh
2. nadwyzka = bilans = produkcja - zuzycie
3. Battery charges from nadwyzka (if capacity available) → reduces nadwyzka
4. Remaining nadwyzka sold at RCE price → counted in sprzedaz_kwh
   ...
5. Peak discharge: rozlad_docelowy = min(zuzycie, moc_rozlad, magazyn_stan)
6. energia_dostarczona = rozlad_docelowy * sprawnosc_rozladowania
7. nadwyzka_uwolniona = min(energia_dostarczona, produkcja) → sold again
```

Step 1 says "PV served zuzycie." Step 7 says "battery freed PV worth `produkcja` so sell it." But `produkcja = zuzycie + bilans`. The `bilans` portion was already sold in step 4. The `zuzycie` portion was already counted as self-consumed in step 1.

Concrete example: `produkcja = 1000 Wh`, `zuzycie = 500 Wh`, `bilans = 500 Wh`.
- Step 1: autokonsumpcja += 500 Wh
- Step 4: sprzedaz += 500 Wh (assuming battery was full)
- Step 7: nadwyzka_uwolniona = min(487 Wh, 1000 Wh) = 487 Wh → sprzedaz += 487 Wh

Total attributed: 500 (autokon) + 500 (surplus) + 487 (freed) = 1487 Wh from 1000 Wh production. Over a year with daily summer peak-hour PV production, this inflates annual revenue materially.

The correct accounting: when battery displaces PV at load, subtract the displaced amount from `autokonsumpcja_kwh` and add it to `sprzedaz_kwh`. Or limit `nadwyzka_uwolniona` to at most `zuzycie` (the portion of production serving load, now freed) minus what was already sold as surplus — i.e., `nadwyzka_uwolniona = min(energia_dostarczona, zuzycie)` and subtract the same from `autokonsumpcja_kwh`.
